I am a PhD student in Sociology at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research examines how family dynamics, structures, and relationships shape the health of individuals within families in the Philippines, with particular attention to health disparities across the country. Alongside this work, I study substance use and mental health.

I am a mixed-methods researcher, drawing on both quantitative and qualitative approaches to understand how social and familial contexts influence health outcomes. My work is grounded in the Filipino context, where I focus on the social determinants of health and the ways family systems can both support and constrain individual well-being
